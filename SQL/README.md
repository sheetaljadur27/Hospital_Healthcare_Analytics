* &#x20;Hospital Patient, Billing \& Operations Analytics
* 
* &#x20;Project Overview
* 
* This project analyzes hospital patient, visit, billing, department, doctor, procedure, and pharmacy data using SQL and Power BI.
* 
* The objective is to transform healthcare data into meaningful KPIs, reports, and business insights that can support hospital operational and financial analysis.
* 
* Business Objectives
* 
* \* Analyze patient visit activity
* \* Compare OPD and IPD utilization
* \* Analyze department-wise visits and revenue
* \* Monitor doctor visit activity
* \* Analyze hospital billing and revenue
* \* Identify monthly revenue trends
* \* Create healthcare KPIs and an interactive dashboard
*
